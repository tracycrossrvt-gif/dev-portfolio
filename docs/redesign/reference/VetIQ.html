VetIQ Analytics · Project summary

Teaching new managers which numbers matter.

A practice manager can open a dozen reports and still not know where to look. VetIQ puts the handful of numbers that run a hospital on one screen, with targets, a forecast and clear next steps. Amerivet now uses it to train new practice managers.

01 · The problem

More data didn't mean more clarity.

Running a hospital comes down to a few levers: revenue, average client transaction, labor, cost of goods and new clients. A new manager usually gets that information spread across reports, with no sense of which number to fix first.

I built VetIQ around the way I actually read a hospital's month: where are we against goal, where will we land, and what do we do about it this week.

02 · Product decisions

Built to answer three questions in order.

01

How healthy is the month?

One Practice Health Score, weighted across revenue, ACT, labor, COGS and client growth, so a new manager knows right away whether to dig in.

02

Where will we land?

A month-end forecast based on business days elapsed shows projected revenue against goal, clearly labeled as an estimate, not booked revenue.

03

What do I do about it?

"Where to focus" flags the metrics off target and pairs them with next steps, like reviewing unused appointment capacity or doctor production.

04

A safe place to practice

The demo workspace uses sample data that trainees can edit, saved only in their browser, so they can test what-ifs without touching a real hospital's numbers.
